Unoffical Apps

Unofficial apps are easy to spot.

Unofficial apps are direct JavaScript files, either made by a developer that hasn't yet been approved, or by someone creating a homebrew app.

Unofficial/homebrew apps aren't necessarily dangerous. Some might just be not-yet approved apps, or homebrew apps made by someone to port something (e.g., a game, dashboard).

Though, this isn't made to discourage homebrew. Homebrew is welcome in the PX0 family.
